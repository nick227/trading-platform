# RP-11 — Research Partner workspace

Research directions now use a persistent, versioned dialogue, with model-assisted interpretation opt-in and user approval required to change the mandate. The workspace separates saving a research question from explicitly running a budgeted investigation. Reports link claim citations to individual source records, preserve raw-source availability indicators and show planning results as unverified hypotheses.

The primary product navigation stays at three destinations; Research Partner remains linked contextually from Bots and is accessible at /research-partners.

All forms are authenticated through the existing RP API. No simulated portfolio controls and no claims of financial performance are added. This is a UI implementation, not a live-deployment verification.
